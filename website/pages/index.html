What RiskMandate is

In riskmandate.ai’s own words

This site says nothing about RiskMandate beyond what riskmandate.ai says. Quoted from that site (read on 8 November 2026, site version 1.40.3; check the site for what it says now):

Give agents access. Not free rein.

Agent Behaviour Policies for the AI agents in Gmail, HR, payroll, CRM and meeting apps: what each one can reach, what you authorised it to do, and the gap between the two.

Know their reach. Define their mandate. Control the gap.

The app is a way to reach that site from a phone or tablet, and a testbed for putting it in front of people on managed devices. It does not run policies and does not claim to.
