## Blocking
**Q-stand-1 (high): the staff corner swallows taps.** `StandHostContent` draws a 48 dp `Box` at `BottomEnd` over the content with `combinedClickable(onClick = {}, onLongClick = ...)`. A clickable consumes every tap in its bounds, so whatever the app or a feature puts in the bottom-end 48 dp square is dead. Probe: a `clickable` 48 dp box in the bottom-end corner, wrapped in `StandHostContent`, receives **0 of 1** taps (`expected 1 but was 0`). It happens **always, with stand mode off too** (the corner is composed regardless), on every screen including Home, Licences and Diagnostics, and for features A to C: a last tab in a bottom bar (feature A has native tabs), a Retry or Next button, the Back/Replay control. At the stand this looks like a broken button.
Fix suggestion: do not put a clickable over the content. Detect the long-press on the outer `Box`'s existing `pointerInput` (Initial pass, never consuming; start a timer on a down event inside the corner rectangle, cancel on up/move beyond slop) or use `pointerInput` with `detectTapGestures(onLongPress = ...)` only on the corner, which leaves a plain tap to pass through (verify with the probe). Keep the TalkBack long-click label.

## Findings (not blocking)
- **Q-stand-2 (medium, brief vs build):** the brief says "on and off from the home screen"; this has no home-screen entry, only a hidden long-press in a corner of any screen. That is a reasonable design for a stand (visitors cannot toggle it) but it is a decision for Dinis, and the 10-minute run now says "long-press the bottom-end corner" (plan row 9 updated). Staff must be told where it is; there is no visible cue while off.
- **Q-stand-3 (medium, claim):** the app now stores a setting (`stand_mode` SharedPreferences: enabled, idle minutes). It collects nothing and the backup rules keep it on the device, but the Mandate view (C) and the privacy text must not say "stores no data": say "stores only the stand-mode settings, on this device". Plan C-4 amended; dev.mandate and play are told through the conductor.
- **Q-stand-4 (low):** stand mode is persisted, so a restart comes back "on" and asks to pin again (once per process; `asked` is saved only across rotation). Fine for a stand, but a tablet put back in a drawer stays in stand mode: staff must switch it off. Documented in the plan.
- **Q-stand-5 (low, test gap):** the idle reset is tested at the `StandHostContent` level with a fake clock and lifecycle (good); not tested: idle reset from a feature route through the real `MainActivity` (`atHome` false because of a feature, Licences, Diagnostics); the corner on top of a real screen (this finding); the dialog under rotation (the `panel` is saved, `enabled`/`minutes` are re-read from settings: looks right, not run). I add the first when a feature is enabled.
- **Q-stand-6 (info):** unused strings `stand_ok` and `stand_cancel`. The pt-PT status line says "o Android foi recusado": better "o pedido ao Android foi recusado"; a Portuguese speaker should read all of `feature_stand.xml` (plan row 3 covers it).
- **Q-stand-7 (info):** `IdleTimer` is clean (monotonic clock, fires once, re-armed by a touch, 1 minute floor); `ActivityScreenLock` wraps every call; unpinning only when this run pinned is the right call. Idle uses `elapsedRealtime`, which keeps counting while the screen is off; with keep-awake on that does not matter.
