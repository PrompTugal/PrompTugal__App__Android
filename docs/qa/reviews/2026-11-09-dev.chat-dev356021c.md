## Blocking enabling
**Q-chat-1 (medium, plan H-8 and X-3): a rotation throws the visitor's conversation off the screen.** `ChatFeature.Content` holds the `ChatController` in plain `remember`, so a rotation (activity recreation) builds a new one and `start()` opens a *new empty chat*, moving the old one to the history list. Probe with `StateRestorationTester`: after sending "hello there" and receiving the answer, **2 messages on screen before the rotation, 0 after**, with 1 conversation saved in history. The conversation is not lost from disk but the visitor sees an empty chat after turning the tablet, and has to open History to find it. Worse, by reading (not run): an answer that is still streaming at that moment is **lost completely**, because its save runs on the composition's scope, which the rotation cancels (`persist()` launches on `scope`; a cancelled scope starts nothing) and the history is otherwise only written at the end of an answer. On a stand tablet that people turn, this reads as the chat forgetting. Fix suggestion: keep the open conversation's id in `rememberSaveable` and have `start()` reselect it when it exists (save after the user's message is sent, and write the partial answer from a `NonCancellable` block when the job ends); or hold the controller in the host like `LlmHost`. The existing tests do not cover it; the probe is the check.

## Decision for Dinis
**Q-chat-2 (security H.3, your call): history persists on a shared stand tablet.** `ChatPolicy.WIPE_ON_LEAVE = false`: what one visitor types stays on the tablet, visible in History to the next visitor, until someone taps Clear. It matches your decision (history kept on the tablet only, with a clear button), and nothing leaves the device, but at a stand the next person sees the last person's questions. `WIPE_ON_LEAVE = true` deletes history whenever the chat leaves the screen (Back or stand mode's idle reset). Recommended for the summit build; the choice is yours.
