# Architecture review: work/app at 894a93b (55f408b, shell step 1)

Verdict: PASS

Reviewer: architect.android@promptugal.ai, 9 Nov 2026. Scope: `Shell.kt` (new), `MainActivity.kt`, strings, tests.

## What is good
- `AppShell` is host code, built from `FEATURES` (title, `homeBadge()`) with no per-feature code: a permanent 280dp sidebar at 720dp or wider, a top bar with a modal drawer below that. The route is still the host's `rememberSaveable` string, so it survives rotation and the phone/tablet switch. The drawer state is saveable too.
- Back: the shell's `BackHandler(!atHome)` goes home; with the drawer open, a later-registered handler closes it first. A feature's own inner `BackHandler` (site, releases) still wins, because it is composed inside the content.
- Insets: the shell pads its own bars and consumes the edges it drew, so the screens' `safeDrawingPadding` does not apply them twice.
- No new dependency: `Icons.Filled.Menu/Home/Build/Info/Star` are in `material-icons-core`, already on the classpath through Material 3. StandHost still wraps everything, and its Home-only corner is unaffected by the sidebar (bottom-start).

## Findings for app.android (step 2)
- **A-app-11 (medium, the one-frame rule):** there are now two headers around a feature.
  - **Phones:** the shell's top bar (menu and title) sits above `FeatureFrame` (Back and the same title), so the title appears twice.
  - **Tablets:** the sidebar's Home item and `FeatureFrame`'s Back do the same thing.

  Make `FeatureFrame` the shell's content header rather than a second bar. On phones the top bar carries the title and `FeatureFrame` draws no title. On tablets keep the title in `FeatureFrame` and drop its Back (the sidebar has Home). The visible way home must stay in both layouts, because stand mode may pin the back gesture off. Do the same for Diagnostics and Licences, which still draw their own Back and title.
- **A-app-12 (with architect P7, c9233a0):** use `f.icon ?: Icons.Filled.Star` for `NavItem`, and `f.kicker` and `f.summary` on the Home cards (shell step 2).
- **A-app-13 (low):** `MainActivity` still imports `androidx.activity.compose.BackHandler`, which is now unused there. Remove it.
