## What is good
- Shared tokens in one place. A status palette that is never colour alone (word plus glyph) and passes AAA. Reduce-motion respected. Components are stateless, take their text from callers, and are tested with screenshots.
- `LocalStatusPalette` and `LocalWideLayout` are read-only CompositionLocals, not global mutable state. Accepted.

## Findings for design.android
- **A-des-1 (HOLD):** `FeatureScaffold` is a second frame: a Back button, the title as heading, and `safeDrawingPadding`. The host already wraps every feature in `FeatureFrame` (app, on dev since 2bbc37b). A feature that uses `FeatureScaffold` gets two Back buttons, two titles and double insets, which is exactly what abpdemo, mandate and site are on HOLD for. There must be one frame. Architect decision:
  - `FeatureFrame` (host, app-owned) stays the only frame. If design wants it to look like `FeatureScaffold` (divider, headlineSmall, padding), give app a `ui/FeatureTitleBar(title, onBack)` and app uses it inside `FeatureFrame`.
  - `FeatureScaffold` becomes a content-only `FeatureBody(scrollable, maxContentWidth, content)`: wide/narrow padding, the reading-width cap, the optional scroll, `LocalWideLayout`. No Back button, no title, no insets.
- **A-des-2 (low):** `UiTokens.WideMinWidth` (840dp) and app's `TwoPaneMinWidth` (640dp) on Home differ. Pick one breakpoint and tell app.
- **A-des-3 (info):** `LayerRow` overlaps with abpdemo's private `LayerRow`. Once abpdemo moves to `ui/LayerRow`, the `ui_layer_<index>` tags replace `abpdemo_layer_*`; agree it with dev.abpdemo.
