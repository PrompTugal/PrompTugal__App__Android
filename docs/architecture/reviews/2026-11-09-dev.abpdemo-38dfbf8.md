## Findings for dev.abpdemo
- **A-abp-1 (HOLD):** the screen draws its own title (the `feature_abpdemo_title` heading) and its own Home button, inside the host's `FeatureFrame` (on dev since 2bbc37b), which already has Back and the title. Result: two titles and two ways home. Remove both; keep the subtitle and the banner. Features draw content only (docs/architecture/README.md).
- **A-abp-2 (HOLD, gate red on the trial merge):** `contentDescription = "$number. $name, $stateText"` is a string template, so it fails `LocalisationTest.noScreenHardcodesText` and `FeatureStringsTest.featureScreensHardcodeNoText`. Use a string resource with arguments (`abpdemo_layer_cd`, `%1$d. %2$s, %3$s`, en and pt-PT).
- **A-abp-3 (medium):** the layout is always two panes (0.42/0.58) with fixed `sp` sizes up to 30sp, and the diagram column does not scroll. On a phone, a 7" tablet in portrait or at 200% font, rows will clip. Use one column below about 840dp of width (`BoxWithConstraints`), and let the diagram scroll.
- **A-abp-4 (medium, cross-feature):** auto-play loops untouched, but stand's idle reset sends the app home after N minutes. Agree the behaviour with dev.stand through the conductor (stand review A-stand-3).
- **A-abp-5 (low):** `surfaceVariantOrSurface()` returns `surfaceVariant` and its comment says otherwise. Delete it or make it true. Hardcoded `sp` and colours: align with design's `ui/` once it lands.
