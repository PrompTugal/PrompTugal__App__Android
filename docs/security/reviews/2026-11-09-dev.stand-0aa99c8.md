## Blocking findings

| Id | Severity | Where | Finding | Fix | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-stand-1 | Medium | `StandSettings.kt` (`hash`, `setPin`, `checkPin`) and `PinGate` | The PIN is `SHA-256(salt + pin)`, 4 to 8 digits. A fast hash over at most 10^8 candidates is broken in seconds (4 digits: instantly) by anyone who can read `stand_mode.xml` (root, a backup, a lost-and-imaged tablet). `PinGate` keeps its failure count and lock time in memory: rotating the screen recreates the activity and the `remember`ed gate, and killing the app does the same, so the "5 tries then 60 s" limit resets and guessing is unlimited. The checklist (section D2) asked for a salted slow hash, a persistent lockout and no plaintext. | Hash with `SecretKeyFactory.getInstance("PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256")`, at least 120 000 iterations, 16-byte random salt (already there), 256-bit output (available on minSdk 26); store iterations with the hash so it can be raised. Keep the failure count and the lock-until time in the same SharedPreferences (use `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime` plus a stored boot count, or wall time and refuse a clock set backwards) so a restart or rotation does not reset them; grow the lockout (1 min, 5 min, 15 min). Or drop the PIN: the status page itself says it "deters visitors; it is not strong security". If it stays, the UI must keep saying so. | dev.stand |
| R-stand-2 | Medium (docs) | privacy and Data safety statements | After this merge the app keeps data on the device: a `stand_mode` SharedPreferences file with a switch, a number of minutes and a salted PIN hash. `privacy.html`, `privacy-policy.md`, `data-safety.md` and `listing.md` say the app "keeps no files, no database and no preferences of its own". That becomes false. Nothing is sent anywhere, so "no data collected" and "none shared" remain true, but the statements must say what is kept. | play updates the four documents in the same merge: the app stores, on the device only, whether stand mode is on, the idle minutes and (if a staff member sets one) a salted hash of a staff PIN; not sent, not backed up, deleted when the app is uninstalled. Add a line to the diagnostics and mandate statements if they list "stored data". Conductor: merge the play change together with this head, or after it, before any store submission. | play, conductor |
| R-stand-3 | Low to Medium | `res/xml/data_extraction_rules.xml` (app), claim in `status/dev.stand.md` | The status page says the PIN file is "excluded from backup and transfer by the existing rules". The rules exclude only `domain="root"`; SharedPreferences live in the `sharedpref` domain, which they do not name. `allowBackup=false` helps, but its effect on Android 12 and later device-to-device transfer, where the rules file governs, is not something I can confirm here. | app edits the rules to exclude every domain explicitly, in both blocks: `<exclude domain="sharedpref" path="."/>`, `file`, `database`, `external`, `root` (this is R-app-1 from the 44131ad review, now with a reason).
