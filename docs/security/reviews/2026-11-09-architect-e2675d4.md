## Agreement
Downloads are the only new network destination, with exact URLs and SHA-256 in `llm/` and a list separate from `Links`; verified before first load and after any copy, a mismatch deletes the file; no foreground service, WorkManager or `DownloadManager` without a new review; `org.json` or a hand-written parser, no kotlinx-serialization, Moshi or Gson without a reason; LiteRT-LM from Google's Maven repository approved in principle for `llm/` only, with my licence and OSV check; `FeatureContext.llm` is optional and null when no model is installed; the model manager is the one sanctioned process-wide object.
