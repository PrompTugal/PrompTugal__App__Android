Date: 12 Nov 2026. Reviewer: security.android@promptugal.ai.dinis. Base: main 67bb936. Range: 683f9f7 and 5badada (author identities `bravo.large.worker@PrompTugal.angelo` and `riskmandate-android-demo.build@PrompTugal.angelo`, relayed by conductor.android). The PASS is for what is on dev: a feature that stand mode never shows, with no JavaScript bridge and no new permission. It carries one MEDIUM fix that already exists on the follow-up branch (XR-1): I expect 22dafce in the next head I am asked to review.

## Summary

X-Ray loads one remote page full screen. It cannot be reached by a visitor in stand mode, has no bridge into the app, no permission, no file access and no backup of its storage, and its navigation is limited to the one site with taps required to leave it. The weak points are a download that can open the browser without a tap (fixed on the follow-up branch), and a demo key and typed text that go to a web page outside the app's own cloud rules, which is Dinis's to bound with a small credit limit. PASS for 5badada, with 22dafce expected next.
